# Privacy Policy — ngrok-inspect-plus

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

`ngrok-inspect-plus` is a Chrome extension that improves the local ngrok agent dashboard.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or
telemetry. It makes no network requests of its own.

## What the extension does with data

- **Preferences.** The extension stores your own settings (theme mode and copy formatting) in
  Chrome's sync storage so they persist across sessions. This data stays in your Chrome profile
  and is never sent to the developer.
- **Dashboard content.** When you click a copy button, the extension reads the request or
  response data that ngrok displays in your local dashboard and writes it to your clipboard. This
  happens only on your explicit click, entirely on your machine. The data is not stored or
  transmitted anywhere.

## Where the extension runs

Only on the ngrok agent dashboard served on loopback
(`http://localhost:<port>` / `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`, default port `4040`). It does not run on,
or read, any other website.

## Third-party services

None. There are no analytics, advertising, or third-party services.
